## Your profile

Open **Settings** and tap your name at the top.

| Field | Who sees it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Everyone | Also saved to your radio as its long name, with initials as its short name. Up to 64 bytes in Pangea, 36 on the radio. |
| **Emoji** | Pangea users | Shown when you have no photo, or before someone downloads it. |
| **Color** | Pangea users | Background for your emoji or initials. |
| **Photo** | Pangea users you share with | See [Profile photos](#profile-photos). |

Changes are saved on your device right away. If your radio isn't connected, your name is written to it and your updated profile is sent out as soon as it's back.

> [!NOTE]
> Saving a new name restarts the radio for a moment. That's normal.

## Seeing other people

Tap someone's avatar or name to open their profile. You'll see:

- Their name, emoji and photo (if they use Pangea), or their radio's name if they don't
- Their code, which you can tap to copy
- **Add to Contacts** / **Remove from Contacts**
- A private **Nickname** field
- **Update Profile Photo**, when a newer photo is available
- **Send Message**

## Profile photos

Photos travel over the radio, so they're kept small and only sent when asked for.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | Resized to 256 × 256 px and compressed to about 14 KB |
| Sending | Only when someone taps **Update Profile Photo** on your profile |
| Download time | A few minutes over LoRa. You can keep chatting meanwhile. |
| At once | Up to 6 photo downloads run at the same time. More wait their turn. |
| Where | Only between people who share a private chat or a private group |

On a Mac you can choose a photo from Finder, from Photos, or drag one onto your avatar.

## Nicknames

A nickname replaces someone's name everywhere in Pangea, for you only. It's never sent to anyone. Their own name still shows under it on their profile.

## Contacts

Save anyone from their profile with **Add to Contacts**.

Saved contacts show up:

- At the top of **New Message**. Tap one to open the chat.
- First in **Invite People** for groups.

Contacts are stored on your device. They stay if you switch or forget your radio, and they keep their name even if their radio drops out of your radio's list.

To remove someone, open their profile and tap **Remove from Contacts**, or long-press them in New Message.

## Who gets your profile

Your name, emoji, color and photo version are sent as a small profile card. Pangea only sends it over encrypted links:

| Recipient | Gets your card |
|---|---|
| People you've messaged privately | Yes |
| Saved contacts | Yes |
| Members of your private groups | Yes |
| Everyone Nearby | **No, never** |
| Groups using a public or default key | **No** |

When you edit your profile, the new card goes to up to 8 people at once, starting with the people you've talked to most recently. Others get it the next time you message them.

Deleting a private chat with someone who isn't a contact or in one of your groups stops your profile updates to them until you message again.
