# Map

The **Map** tab shows radios that have shared their location, along with your own location. It's the first tab, before **Chats** and **Settings**.

- [Reading the map](#reading-the-map)
- [Seeing who's there](#seeing-whos-there)
- [Why a radio isn't on the map](#why-a-radio-isnt-on-the-map)
- [Map in Expert Mode](#map-in-expert-mode)

## Reading the map

- Each radio with a location is a colored circle with its short name.
- Radios that only share an approximate location get a see-through circle showing the area they could be in.
- Your own location is the blue dot.
- Pinch to zoom and drag to move around. The map opens where you last left it.
- The button in the bottom-right corner switches between **Standard**, **Hybrid** and **Satellite** maps.
- With VoiceOver on, each radio reads as the person's name. A group of radios reads as how many are there; double-tap to zoom in.

## Seeing who's there

Tap a radio to open that person's profile. From there you can:

- **Send Message** to start a private chat.
- **Add to Contacts** so they're easy to find later.
- Add a nickname only you can see.

When several radios are at the same spot, tap the stack to pick one from a list.

Tapping your own radio doesn't open anything.

## Why a radio isn't on the map

- **It has no GPS, or location is off.** Many radios never share a location, so they never appear. You can still message them.
- **It hasn't sent its location yet.** Radios that share it usually send it every 15 to 30 minutes.
- **It's out of range.** The map only shows radios your radio has heard.

If no radio has shared a location yet, the map says so at the top.

## Map in Expert Mode

Expert Mode adds more buttons along the bottom of the map: filters, map options like offline maps and terrain, a legend, and coverage estimates. It also shows waypoints and lets you drop them, shows trace routes, and opens full node details when you tap a radio. While the map is open in Expert Mode, the screen stays on. See [Map & Waypoints](expert/map.md).
