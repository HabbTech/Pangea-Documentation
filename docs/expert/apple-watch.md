# Apple Watch

Pangea comes with a small Apple Watch app. It helps you find a radio on foot, which is called a foxhunt. It doesn't send or show messages.

The Watch app gets its node list from Pangea on your iPhone. The Watch doesn't connect to your radio itself.

## What you need

| | |
|---|---|
| Apple Watch | Paired with your iPhone. The Watch app installs with Pangea. |
| iPhone | Pangea open and connected to a radio |
| Location | Location Services on for the Watch app |
| Range | Watch and iPhone close enough to talk to each other |

## Foxhunt

Swipe up or down on the Watch to move between **Foxhunt** and **Phone**.

**Foxhunt** lists radios within ½ mile (about 800 m) of you that have shared a location. Radios you've picked to hunt are always at the top, however far away they are.

Each row shows the radio's short name, its long name, how far away it is, and a small arrow pointing toward it.

The distance color works like hot and cold:

| Distance color | Means |
|---|---|
| Red | Close, under about 270 m (⅙ mile) |
| Yellow | Middle, about 270 to 540 m |
| Blue | Far, over about 540 m (⅓ mile) |

If the list says **No nearby nodes**, no radio with a location is within ½ mile.

## The compass

Tap a radio to open its compass.

- The arrow points at the radio. The dial turns as you turn.
- The middle shows your heading, the direction to the radio, and the distance.
- On the compass the distance turns green when you're close, yellow in the middle, and red when you're far.
- Your Watch taps your wrist when you're facing within 10° of the radio, so you don't have to keep looking.

Walk until the arrow points straight ahead, and keep going until the distance is close to zero.

The distance is only as good as the radio's last reported location. Radios usually send their location every 15 to 30 minutes, so a moving radio may not be where the Watch says.

## Phone

**Phone** shows whether the Watch can reach Pangea on your iPhone.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Phone Connected** | Working. Shows how many nodes it has. |
| **Phone Not Reachable** | The iPhone is out of range or Pangea isn't running. The Watch keeps using the last list it got. |

Tap **Refresh** to get a new list.

## Picking a radio to hunt

On your iPhone, with Expert Mode on:

1. Open the **Nodes** tab and tap the radio.
2. Under **Actions**, tap **Foxhunt on your watch**.

The radio is pinned to the top of the Watch's Foxhunt list. The button only shows when a Watch is paired.
