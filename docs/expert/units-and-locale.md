# Units & Language

Pangea shows temperatures, distances, speeds, dates and times the way your device is set up. There's no separate setting in Pangea.

- [Changing units](#changing-units)
- [What changes](#what-changes)
- [What never changes](#what-never-changes)
- [Dates and times](#dates-and-times)

## Changing units

| Device | Where |
|---|---|
| iPhone / iPad | **Settings → General → Language & Region** |
| Mac | **System Settings → General → Language & Region** |

| Setting | What it changes in Pangea |
|---|---|
| **Temperature** | °C or °F |
| **Measurement System** | Metric, US or UK: meters and kilometers, or feet and miles |
| **Date Format**, **Calendar**, **First Day of Week** | How dates are shown |
| **Number Format** | Decimal point and digit grouping |

Pangea picks up the change right away. You don't need to restart it.

Radios always send their readings in metric. Pangea converts them for display.

## What changes

| Reading | Metric | US |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 22 °C | 72 °F |
| Short distance | 350 m | 1,148 ft |
| Long distance | 2.5 km | 1.6 mi |
| Altitude | 1,200 m | 3,937 ft |
| Speed | 12 km/h | 7 mph |
| Wind | 5 m/s | 11 mph |
| Weight | 24.5 kg | 54.0 lbs |
| Rain | 12 mm | 0.5 in |

Short distances stay in meters or feet. Longer ones switch to kilometers or miles on their own.

You'll see these in the node list, node details, sensor readings and charts, the map, and the compass.

## What never changes

| Reading | Always shown in |
|---|---|
| Air pressure | hPa |
| Heading and bearing | Degrees |
| Radiation | µR/hr |
| Coordinates | Decimal degrees |
| Humidity, battery, soil moisture | % |
| Signal | dB and dBm |

## Dates and times

Every time and date in Pangea follows your device: message times, last heard, logs and chart axes. That includes 24-hour time if you have it on.

Recent times show as "5 min ago" or "2 hours ago", in your device's language.

The **Display Fahrenheit** setting under **Settings → Telemetry** only changes the radio's own screen, not Pangea.
