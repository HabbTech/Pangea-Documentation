# FAQ

**Do I need cell service or Wi-Fi?**
No. Messages go radio to radio over LoRa. Your phone only needs Bluetooth to talk to your radio.

**Does it cost anything to send messages?**
No. There's no plan, account or subscription. You need a radio.

**How far does it reach?**
Anywhere from a few hundred meters in town to 10 km or more with line of sight. Radios in between relay messages farther. See [Limits](limits.md#typical-range).

**Can people using other Meshtastic apps message me?**
Yes. Pangea uses the Meshtastic protocol. They see your name and messages. Photos and emoji only show between Pangea users.

**Who can read my messages?**
Private messages: only the two of you. Groups: people with the invite. Everyone Nearby: anyone in range. See [Privacy & Security](privacy-and-security.md).

**Why did my code change?**
Your code comes from your radio. A different radio means a different code.

**Can I use one radio with two phones?**
Yes, but only one can be connected at a time.

**Can I use Pangea on my Mac and iPhone at the same time?**
Each needs its own connection to a radio. One radio talks to one device at a time, so use two radios, or switch between them.

**Why don't I see the Nodes, Map or Connect tabs?**
They're part of [Expert Mode](settings.md#expert-mode).

**Why is there a limit of 7 groups?**
The radio holds 8 channels, and one is always Everyone Nearby.

**What happens to my chats if I switch radios?**
**Forget This Radio** clears that radio's chats from your device. Your contacts and profile stay. Pairing a different radio starts its chats fresh.

**Is there an Android or Windows version?**
Both are in development. See [Platform status](../README.md#platform-status).

**Is Pangea open source?**
Pangea is built on the open Meshtastic protocol and firmware. The app itself isn't public yet.
